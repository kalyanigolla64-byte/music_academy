ABOUT THE OWNER

Guiding Kalyani
with music and tradition.

Owner: Kalyani

Kalyani Indian Traditional Music Academy was founded with the vision of preserving and sharing the beauty of Indian traditional music. The academy provides a welcoming space where students can learn with sincerity, discipline and joy.

Guided by respect for the Guru–Shishya tradition, the academy encourages regular practice, strong musical foundations and personal expression.

ॐ Music is a lifelong sadhana.

OUR STORY

A place where tradition lives

At Kalyani Indian Traditional Music Academy, music is more than a subject to study—it is a cultural heritage to experience. We bring together traditional teaching, regular practice and performance opportunities so that students can understand both the discipline and the joy of Indian music.

From a beginner taking the first musical step to an advanced learner preparing for a stage performance, every student is encouraged to grow at a comfortable pace while developing a deep respect for the art form.

THE FOUNDER'S MESSAGE

Learning with heart, discipline and devotion

“I believe every learner has a unique musical voice. My aim is to create an environment where students feel confident to learn, practise and perform while staying connected to the roots of Indian tradition.”

Through Kalyani Indian Traditional Music Academy, the goal is to make authentic Indian classical and traditional music accessible to young learners and music enthusiasts. We value patience, consistency and the bond between teacher and student.

What we believe

Good music education develops the whole person. It builds concentration, patience, listening skills, confidence and an appreciation for culture.

Our classrooms encourage questions, careful listening, regular riyaz and respectful interaction between Gurus and students.

OUR TEACHING APPROACH

From learning to performance

Our approach combines traditional values with a friendly, structured learning experience.

01

Strong Foundations

Students begin with swara, tala, rhythm, listening and basic musical concepts before moving to advanced lessons.

02

Regular Riyaz

Consistent practice is encouraged so that technique becomes natural and musical confidence grows step by step.

03

Stage Confidence

Students receive opportunities to participate in academy events, cultural programs and performances.
